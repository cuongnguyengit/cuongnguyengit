- 🛡️ **Now** — Senior AI Engineer at **VinSOC (Vingroup)**: agentic RAG chatbot and executive risk reporting for an enterprise cybersecurity platform.

- 🔊 **Speech** — Vietnamese TTS research and production systems (VLSP TTS Third Prize 2021 & 2023).
- 🤖 **Side projects** — gateways and tooling that make LLM / TTS providers interchangeable, and agentic workflows on Claude Code.
